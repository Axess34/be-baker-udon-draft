About Be Baker

A bakery café in Udon Thani for custom birthday cakes, coffee, and soft pastel moments.

BE BAKER

Life is uncertain. Eat cake first.

We’re a neighbourhood bakery café on Soi Pracha Uthit 2 in Mak Khaeng — known for cute custom-order birthday cakes (เค้กวันเกิดตามสั่ง) and a Korean-style mint café look.

From soft pastel tiers to character cakes with your message on top, we bake for birthdays and special days. Come for coffee and cake, or order ahead and pick up when you’re ready.

  • Custom birthday cakes made to order
  • Mint façade, chocolate accents, pastel cakes
  • Coffee, latte, americano & café drinks
  • Open daily — confirm hours by phone or Facebook
  • ~29,000 followers on Facebook · BEBAKERRRR

The shop

A cute stop opposite Flyflow

Follow us on Facebook for new cake designs, café updates, and order details. We’re happy to help you plan the right size and theme for your celebration.
